# Play-along lead sheets

Single-page, play-along melodies with a bouncing ball that lands on each note at the moment it should sound.

**Live pages:**
- *The Sound of Silence* (words and music by Paul Simon), E minor: https://nickyfoto.github.io/sound-of-silence/
- *Mystical Magical* (words and music by Benson Boone, Jackson Lafrantz Larsen, Steve Kipner, Terry Shaddick and Evan Blair), C major: https://nickyfoto.github.io/sound-of-silence/mystical-magical/
- *Jingle Bells* (James Lord Pierpont, easy guitar arrangement by Peter Edvinsson), G major, with guitar tab: https://nickyfoto.github.io/sound-of-silence/jingle-bells/

What each page does:
- The current note is highlighted while the ball moves from note to note.
- Tempo slider, count-in, metronome, chord backing, loop, and a choice of melody instrument.
- Click any note to jump there. Keyboard: `Space` play/pause, `←` `→` previous/next bar, `R` restart.
- Bar numbers and line breaks follow the printed lead sheet. Lyrics are not included.
- Repeats and road maps are played as written: *The Sound of Silence* repeats bars 18–33; *Mystical Magical* follows its D.S. al Coda, and bars 15, 17 and 18 play different notes the second time; *Jingle Bells* plays its verse and its chorus twice each, taking the 1st ending and then the 2nd.

Tempo:
- The original Simon & Garfunkel recording runs at about 107 bpm; that page starts a little slower, at 96, for practice.
- Benson Boone's recording runs at about 120 bpm, in B major (a half step below the sheet); that page starts at 100.
- *Jingle Bells* is in cut time (two half-note beats to the bar), but its tempo, counter and metronome count quarter notes; it starts at 120. Its melody sounds an octave below the written notes, where a guitar plays it.

Built with [abcjs](https://github.com/paulrosen/abcjs) (notation and audio), loaded from a CDN, so the pages need an internet connection.
